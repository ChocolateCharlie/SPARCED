## M15 Guideline Compliance

While not mandatory in academic settings, SPARCED integrates key principles from
_Section 3: Model Evaluation_ of the ICH M15 guideline to support model
**verification** and **validation**, aiming to bridge the gap between academic
modeling practices and the regulatory expectations of the pharmaceutical industry.
This implementation focuses on enhancing model transparency, traceability, and
documentation quality.

To facilitate compliance, SPARCED provides structured documentation templates
and helper tools that guide users through the process of recording essential
modeling decisions. These include data selection, transformation, and imputation
steps, as well as the identification of key assumptions and evaluation of
scenario plausibility. Users can generate much of the required documentation
**automatically** via the command-line interface, with minimal manual input.

Although SPARCED does not enforce compliance through automated validation
pipelines—preserving flexibility for academic users ; it offers a robust
foundation for industry-aligned modeling workflows. Version tracking, clean
data management practices, and built-in support for sensitivity and data
analysis further strengthen the model evaluation process.

As the M15 guideline continues to evolve, SPARCED will adapt accordingly,
with future support potentially extending to additional regulatory
frameworks (e.g., ICH E6), depending on community and industry engagement.
